Rolex Sapphire Covered Obsidian Dial

Description

A luxury Rolex Daytona chronograph crafted in 18k white gold, featuring a silver sheen obsidian dial and a bezel set with blue sapphires. The Rolex Cosmograph Daytona 126599TSA is an ultra-exclusive Daytona featuring a 40mm 18k white gold case, a silver sheen obsidian stone dial, a bezel set with 36 baguette-cut blue sapphires, and diamond-set case sides. Combining Rolex's legendary chronograph functionality with high jewelry craftsmanship, this off-catalogue model is among the rarest and most collectible modern Daytonas.
