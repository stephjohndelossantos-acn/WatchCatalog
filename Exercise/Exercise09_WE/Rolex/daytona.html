Description

The Rolex Cosmograph Daytona is an iconic luxury sports chronograph originally designed for professional racing drivers. Featuring a 40 mm Oystersteel case, a black Cerachrom tachymeter bezel, and Rolex's in-house Caliber 4131 automatic movement, it delivers exceptional precision, reliability, and a 72-hour power reserve. Its distinctive three-register dial allows accurate timekeeping of racing events while maintaining the timeless elegance and durability that have made the Daytona one of the most coveted watches in the world.
