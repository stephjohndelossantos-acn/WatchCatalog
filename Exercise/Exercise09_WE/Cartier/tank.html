Description

The Cartier Tank is a distinguished dress watch known for its elegant design and precision. Featuring a 33 mm stainless steel case, a fixed bezel, and Cartier's Caliber 1917 MC quartz movement, it offers accurate timekeeping and sophisticated style. Celebrated for its iconic rectangular case, luxurious finish, and association with refined watchmaking, the Tank remains a favorite among watch enthusiasts and collectors worldwide.
