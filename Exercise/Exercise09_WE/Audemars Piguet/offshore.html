Description

The Audemars Piguet Royal Oak Offshore Chronograph is the bold and sportier evolution of the classic Royal Oak, featuring a larger case, more aggressive styling, and advanced chronograph functionality. First introduced in 1993, the Offshore collection earned the nickname "The Beast" due to its oversized design and commanding wrist presence. Powered by the in-house Caliber 4401 automatic flyback chronograph movement, the 43 mm Royal Oak Offshore combines high-performance engineering, exceptional finishing, and modern luxury sports watch aesthetics, making it a favorite among enthusiasts seeking a more robust Royal Oak experience.
